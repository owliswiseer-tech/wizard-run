How to play

  • 👆 Swipe left or right to change lanes
  • ⬆️ Swipe up to jump, ⬇️ swipe down to slide

  • ⛓️ NEW: cursed iron gates can’t be blasted. Dodge them by changing lanes
  • 🪙 Collect galleons and cauldrons for rewards
  • ⚠️ A glowing lane means a curse is coming. Leave it
  • 🦌 When dementors come, tap PATRONUS
  • ⚔️ In a wand duel, tap CAST as fast as you can
